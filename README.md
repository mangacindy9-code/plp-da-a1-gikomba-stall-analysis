## Business problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she has recorded daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough stock record. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. This project uses core Python only (no pandas or NumPy) to answer those questions from 14 days of sales and a five-product stock list.

## Top three findings
1. **Growth:** week 2 sales (KES 24,400) were **10.66% higher** than week 1 (KES 22,050).
2. **Weekends matter most:** weekend days average KES 4,900 versus KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is **82.5% higher**.
3. **Restock now:** Tomatoes and Sukuma are below reorder level, and Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.9%**.

Other numbers: total sales KES 46,450; average KES 3,317.86 per day; best day Saturday of week 2 (KES 5,600); worst day Tuesday of week 1 (KES 1,950); 4 Strong, 4 Normal and 6 Weak days.
